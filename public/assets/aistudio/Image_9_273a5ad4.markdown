## Brand & Style
The design system positions an intelligent beauty advisor as an intimate, trustworthy botanical apothecary paired with dermatological science. Replacing high-intensity clinical teals and harsh digital whites with calm, therapeutic sage and organic eucalyptus tones, the interface evokes a restorative sanctuary. The emotional response is centered on sensory relief, quiet confidence, and clarity, neutralizing digital fatigue during extended skincare consultations and ingredient analysis sessions.

The aesthetic fuses **Soft Modernism** with **Tactile Organic Warmth**:
- Generous breathable whitespace rendered on low-glare, warm herbal-tinted backgrounds.
- High typographic discipline with high-contrast structural text grounded in deep slate-pine rather than pure black.
- Subtle, delicate physical qualities: frosted botanical glass overlays, micro-borders with low opacity, and soft, natural ambient lighting that mimics diffuse botanical garden light.

## Colors
The color architecture rejects aggressive digital saturation in favor of a photopic-friendly, dermatological spectrum:

- **Primary (`#205B54`)**: Deep Pine Sage. Anchor for essential interactive states, main calls-to-action, active diagnostic selectors, and primary brand indicators.
- **Secondary (`#4D7F75`)**: Muted Eucalyptus. Used for secondary navigation, pill fills, supporting iconography, and contextual badges.
- **Tertiary (`#BCE3D8`)**: Hydro-Mint Tint. Reserved for micro-highlights, AI diagnostic glowing pulses, progress completions, and soothing tag surfaces.
- **Neutral Core (`#162825`)**: Slate Pine. Replaces harsh `#000000` to prevent visual jarring and provide high-contrast legibility across headlines and critical data.
- **Surface & Canvas (`#F7FAF8` to `#FBFDFB`)**: Low-glare, warm off-white canvas engineered specifically to mitigate retina exhaustion across prolonged consultations.
- **Surface Containers (`#EDF4F1` to `#E3EDE9`)**: Subtle herbal-tinted containers providing soft segmentation without requiring stark contrast dividers.

## Typography
The pairing establishes a dialogue between botanical editorial sophistication and clean scientific transparency:

- **Headlines (Epilogue)**: Imparts structural elegance with architectural, warm proportions. Used across diagnostic summaries, routine titles, and key conversational queries.
- **Body & Labels (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Delivers clear, welcoming legibility across multi-step regimen breakdowns, ingredient listings, and analytical data charts.
- **Scaling & Hierarchy**: Large screen displays compress seamlessly into `-mobile` variants to maintain immediate conversational immersion without occupying critical viewport height on handheld devices.
- **Weight Pairing**: Body text avoids thin weights (300) to keep legibility effortless against soft herbal surfaces; primary narrative text stays at weight 400 with 600-weighted anchors for skimming.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid-responsive responsive model anchored by an 8pt baseline rhythm:

- **Canvas & Grids**:
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column fluid grid, 16px margins, 16px gutters. Optimized for single-hand chat interfaces and product recommendation carousels.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8-column layout, 32px margins, 24px gutters. Splits into chat stream and real-time skin report panels.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12-column fixed-max layout (max-width 1360px), 48px margins, 32px gutters. Displays dual-panel workflows: AI conversational timeline on the left, interactive regime builder and skin diagnostic metrics on the right.
- **Rhythm & Breathing Room**:
  - Components utilize internal padding derived strictly from `space-sm` (8px) for dense tags up to `space-xl` (40px) for diagnostic hero containers.
  - Avoid dense nesting. Visual breathing room reinforces a calm spa-like atmosphere.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation mimics soft, naturally lit physical layers rather than simulated dark drop-shadows:

- **Tonal Layering**: Primary depth relies on step-up tinted surfaces:
  - Base Layer: `#F7FAF8` (Aura Canvas)
  - Card Level 1: `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border of `#E1EBE7`
  - Card Level 2 (Floating Modals / Flyouts): `#FFFFFF`
- **Botanical Glassmorphism**: Floating panels (bottom navigation bar, AI chat prompt bar, sticky routine actions) use `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` with an 85% opacity surface of `#FAFDFB` and a micro-border of `rgba(32, 91, 84, 0.08)`.
- **Diffuse Ambient Shadows**:
  - `elevation-low`: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(22, 40, 37, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(22, 40, 37, 0.02)`
  - `elevation-mid`: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(22, 40, 37, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(22, 40, 37, 0.03)`
  - `elevation-high`: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(22, 40, 37, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(22, 40, 37, 0.03)`
  Shadows are universally tinted with pine slate (`rgba(22, 40, 37)`) to avoid cold, muddy gray artifacts.

## Shapes
A roundedness level of `2` (8px base corner radius) balances clinical precision with natural softness:

- **Base Components**: Inputs, chat bubbles, alert panels, and cards utilize standard roundedness (8px / `0.5rem`).
- **Interactive Capsules**: Buttons, pill tags, routine steps, and quick-reply AI prompts scale to `rounded-xl` or full pills (`9999px`) to reinforce tactile friendliness and ease of touch.
- **Product & Camera Scan Frames**: Utilize `rounded-lg` (16px / `1rem`) for viewport apertures and cosmetic container mockups, matching the gentle contours of modern skincare packaging.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Deep Pine Sage background (`#205B54`), crisp white text, full pill curvature. On hover: subtle shift to `#184742` with elevation-low.
- **Secondary**: Soft Eucalyptus tint background (`#E8F2EF`), text in `#205B54`, border-free.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Transparent background, text in `#205B54`, 1px border in `#CFE0DB`.
- **AI Assist Action**: Gradient-fill from `#205B54` to `#356E65`, accompanied by a Tertiary Mint sparkle icon.

### Chips & Quick Prompts
- Quick-reply prompts ("Dry skin routine", "Retinol safety", "Fragrance-free") feature a `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px border of `#DCE8E3`, and text in `#3E5652`. Active chips fill with `#E8F2EF` and border shifts to `#205B54`.

### Input Fields & Search
- Chat and search bars use a frosted capsule form factor: `#FFFFFF` fill, inset 1px outline in `#DEE9E5`, inner placeholder styled in `#758F8A`. Focused states transition smoothly to an outline of `#205B54` with a 3px soft focus ring of `rgba(188, 227, 216, 0.45)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom botanical check controls: Unchecked state uses a 1.5px border of `#A8BFB8` with an off-white fill. Checked state smoothly blooms into `#205B54` with an organic check mark in `#FFFFFF`.

### Cards & Diagnostic Panels
- **Skin Analysis Card**: Pure white background, 1px perimeter border in `#E5EFEA`, padded with `space-md` (16px).
- **Match Metric Bar**: A progress track in `#E8F2EF` with a fill of `#205B54` transitioning into `#BCE3D8` for match compatibility percentages.
- **Ingredient Alert Cards**: Clean badges using pale warning tones wrapped in calming eucalyptus borders to avoid alarmist red styling.

### AI Conversational Bubbles
- **User Message**: Solid `#205B54` background with crisp white typography, anchored to the right with smoothed asymmetrical corners.
- **Advisor Response**: Crisp `#FFFFFF` card with a 1px border of `#E2ECE8`, text in `#162825`, anchored with a mini-avatar badge featuring a subtle `#BCE3D8` botanical pulse ring.
